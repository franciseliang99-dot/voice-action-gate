Voice Action Gate

An irreversible action runs only if every one of its arguments was actually spoken. Anything the gate cannot decide is a block.

1 · What you said

idle

Try: send five hundred US dollars to Alice — then try it again saying just dollars. A bare “dollars” does not say which dollars, so the gate refuses to invent one.

If you use the microphone: your audio is streamed from this page directly to AssemblyAI for transcription, using a short-lived token. This demo does not record or store it, and it does not pass through this site’s server. AssemblyAI’s handling of it is governed by their privacy policy. The sample buttons need no microphone.
